### 2. Negotiating the room always scores as the worst decision (medium; design question)

`room-booked` (content-table.md:311–315) gives every response P = 0 and R = 0, so utility is
only the minute cost: hear (keep 60, 5 min) = −H, timebox (45, 1 min) = −0.2H, park
(45, 2 min) = −0.4H. Utility cannot see the booking end, so the only response that protects
the hour is strictly the worst at every headcount. A player who negotiates gets q = 0 for
that decision. Greedy, Reckless and Lookahead (whose next-turn term also ignores the
deadline) will always accept :45. The design (line 76, roster line 393) presents negotiation
as a real choice, and title 9 exists so good play is not punished. This is a
decision-quality property of the content, not a formula bug, and the docs do not say whether
it is intended. CLAUDE.md rule 10 says to settle that in the docs, not in 07b.

Options for Harry:

- **Keep it (the trap is the joke).** Add one sentence to the table saying negotiation is
  intentionally scored as the costliest option. 08a will show the effect on win rates.
- **Price negotiation at 1 minute.** It then ties with timebox (both q = 1, park q = 0), and
  Greedy's tie-break (minutes, then ASCII ID: `hear` < `timebox`) keeps 60. That is a
  content-number change, so it needs Harry's re-approval of that row under D18.

### 3. Legal and the VP have no use once they join (medium–low; design/content conflict)

No response in the table requires `legal` or `vp`, and there is no `legal-absent` reason key.
The design's attendee table says releasing Legal means "legal events lose their best
response" (design-doc-v2.md:93), and the escalation instinct is "Use them or release them"
(line 129). In this table the only legal event is the one that admits Legal, so the claim is
vacuous and "use them" never applies. Each arrival is a pure cost, and releasing it at once is
always right. That may be fine as satire, but the design and table disagree. Either change
design line 93 to "no downside after joining" and note that escalations are a headcount tax,
or author one response that requires Legal (for example on `press-date` or `p1-bug`, with a
`legal-absent` key) and get it re-approved.

### 4. `stand-clear` hear authors a risk change that can never apply (low; data)

`where-we-stand` selects `stand-risk` whenever R ≥ 1 (line 194), so the base `stand-clear`
presentation is only drawn at R = 0, and nothing between draw and resolution can raise R.
Its hear response's R = −1 (line 198) always clamps to 0. It is harmless to the engine, but
the authored `effect.risks` will be transcribed and is easy to show by mistake instead of
the effective `risksDelta`. Set it to 0, matching its outcome line, which already makes no
risk claim. Re-approval is trivial because the effective result does not change.

### 5. Two non-parking responses are tagged `park` (low; title and moment semantics)

`room-booked` "Promise to wrap up" (line 315) and `vp-joins` "Save questions for later"
(line 300) use technique `park`. `parkedCount` counts park actions (tech-spec.md:175), so
both count toward Parking Lot Attendant. They can also become the results card's "first
parked item" moment, and Always-park prefers them. The VP one is defensible (its line puts
questions in the parking lot). The room booking one is not a parked item. Confirm it is
intended, or retag it `timebox` with a distinct response ID.

### 6. Hand-check evidence is not reproducible from the repo (low; test gap for 07b)
